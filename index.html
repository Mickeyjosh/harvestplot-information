HarvestPlot

Nigerian agricultural marketplace

HarvestPlot is being developed to make it easier for farmers, agricultural sellers, traders and buyers across Nigeria to find one another and participate in agricultural trade.

Visit the official HarvestPlot website

What is HarvestPlot?

HarvestPlot is a Nigerian agricultural marketplace focused on connecting people who produce and sell agricultural goods with people looking to buy them. The platform is intended to help agricultural sellers reach more potential customers while giving buyers a clearer way to discover available produce.

Coming soon: HarvestPlot is being prepared as a digital marketplace for agricultural trade. This information page is separate from the official HarvestPlot website.

Who is HarvestPlot for?

Farmers

Create a presence for agricultural products and reach potential buyers beyond the immediate local market.

Agricultural sellers and traders

Present available agricultural goods to a wider audience and connect with people looking to buy.

Buyers

Discover agricultural products from sellers and compare available options before making a purchase.

The idea behind HarvestPlot

Agricultural trade depends on strong connections between producers, sellers, logistics and buyers. HarvestPlot is being built around the idea that technology can make those connections easier to discover and manage.

The goal is simple: help agricultural products find markets and help buyers find the agricultural goods they need.
