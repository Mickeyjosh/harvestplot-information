Returns, Refunds & Cancellations Policy

Our approach to order cancellations, returns, refunds and marketplace disputes.

Effective date: September 3, 2026

HarvestPlot is an agricultural marketplace connecting buyers with farmers, agricultural sellers and traders. Because agricultural products can vary in nature, quantity, quality and perishability, returns and refunds are handled according to the circumstances of each order and the applicable product and delivery conditions.

1. Order cancellation

A buyer may request cancellation before an order has been dispatched. Whether cancellation is possible after dispatch depends on the product, delivery status and circumstances of the order. Perishable or time-sensitive orders may have limited cancellation options once preparation or dispatch has begun.

2. When a return may be accepted

A return or refund request may be considered where a product is materially different from its listing or agreed order, is damaged on delivery, is missing, is incorrectly supplied, or otherwise fails to meet the agreed order requirements.

3. Perishable agricultural products

For perishable goods, buyers should report a problem as soon as possible after delivery, preferably within 24 hours, because quality and condition can change quickly. Photographs, delivery information and other relevant evidence may be requested.

4. Non-perishable products

For non-perishable agricultural products, buyers should report a return or refund issue within 3 days of delivery unless a different period is stated for the specific product or order.

5. Items that cannot reasonably be returned

Some goods may not be suitable for physical return because of their perishable nature, hygiene or safety considerations, contamination risk, processing, or other product characteristics. In such cases, HarvestPlot may assess the evidence and determine an appropriate refund or other resolution without requiring physical return.

6. Refund decisions

Refund requests are reviewed using the order details, product listing, communications, delivery information and evidence provided by the buyer and vendor. Where a refund is approved, it may be full or partial depending on the circumstances. Refunds for payments made through Paystack will be processed through the applicable payment/refund mechanism.

7. Refund timing

The time for a refund to reach a buyer can depend on the payment method and financial institution. Paystack's published support guidance says customers can expect a refund within 3 to 10 working days after a refund is initiated, depending on the applicable process.

8. Vendor responsibility

Vendors are responsible for accurately describing their products, fulfilling accepted orders and cooperating with legitimate return, refund and dispute requests. HarvestPlot may take action where a vendor repeatedly fails to meet marketplace requirements.

9. Disputes

If a buyer and vendor disagree about an order, HarvestPlot may review the available evidence and communicate with both parties to reach an appropriate marketplace resolution. This process does not prevent either party from exercising any rights available under applicable law.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as HarvestPlot develops its marketplace operations and as applicable requirements change. The current version published on this page applies to future orders.
