HARVESTPLOT

Shipping & Delivery Policy

How agricultural orders are prepared and delivered.

Effective date: September 3, 2026

HarvestPlot is a marketplace connecting buyers with farmers, agricultural sellers and traders. HarvestPlot is not a fulfilment centre. Products are generally prepared by the vendor and shipped to the buyer, with delivery potentially facilitated through third-party logistics providers.

1. Order preparation

Vendors are responsible for preparing purchased products for dispatch, packaging them appropriately for the product type, and making them available for collection or shipment within the agreed timeframe.

2. Delivery arrangements

Delivery arrangements may depend on the product, quantity, destination, availability of logistics providers and the agreement applicable to the order. Where third-party logistics are used, delivery is subject to the logistics provider's operational conditions.

3. Buyer information

Buyers must provide accurate delivery information, including the correct name, telephone number and delivery address or other information requested for fulfilment. Incorrect or incomplete information may cause delays or additional delivery arrangements.

4. Perishable and time-sensitive products

Some agricultural products are perishable or time-sensitive. Buyers should be available to receive such orders promptly. Vendors and logistics providers should use appropriate packaging and handling for the nature of the product. Delivery times for perishable products may vary according to distance, weather, logistics availability and product characteristics.

5. Delivery problems

If an order is delayed, damaged, missing or delivered incorrectly, the buyer should report the issue through the HarvestPlot support process as soon as reasonably possible and provide relevant evidence, such as photographs or delivery information where available.

6. Failed delivery

If delivery cannot be completed because a buyer provides incorrect information, is unavailable to receive the order, or otherwise prevents delivery, the parties may need to agree on a new delivery arrangement. Additional logistics costs, where applicable, may be considered according to the circumstances of the order.

7. Responsibility for delivery

Vendors remain responsible for fulfilling orders they accept. HarvestPlot may facilitate marketplace coordination and logistics connections, but the specific delivery arrangement for an order may involve independent third-party logistics providers.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as HarvestPlot develops its marketplace and logistics processes. The current version published on this page applies to future orders.
