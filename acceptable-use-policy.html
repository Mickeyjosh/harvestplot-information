Acceptable Use Policy

Rules for responsible use of the HarvestPlot agricultural marketplace.

Effective date: September 3, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy explains the types of activity permitted on HarvestPlot. HarvestPlot is being developed as a marketplace connecting farmers, agricultural sellers, traders and buyers in Nigeria.

1. Acceptable use

Users must use HarvestPlot lawfully, honestly and responsibly. Listings, messages, orders, payments and other information must be accurate and must not mislead other users.

2. Vendor responsibilities

Vendors are responsible for the products they list, the accuracy of their descriptions, their ability to fulfil orders, and compliance with applicable laws and requirements. Vendors may be required to provide identity, business and bank-account information for verification.

3. Prohibited activity

  • Illegal goods, services or transactions.
  • Counterfeit, stolen or fraudulently obtained goods.
  • False, deceptive or materially misleading product information.
  • Fraud, impersonation, identity theft or attempts to manipulate transactions.
  • Using HarvestPlot to launder money or evade payment, tax, regulatory or legal obligations.
  • Products whose sale requires a licence, permit or regulatory approval where the required approval has not been obtained.
  • Abuse, harassment, threats or unlawful discrimination against other users.
  • Attempts to compromise, disrupt or gain unauthorised access to the platform or another user's account.

4. Agricultural products

Products listed on HarvestPlot must be lawful to sell and suitable for the intended marketplace transaction. Vendors must comply with applicable food, agricultural, environmental, health, safety and other requirements relevant to their products.

5. Verification and enforcement

HarvestPlot may review listings, user activity and verification information and may restrict, suspend or remove accounts or listings where there is suspected fraud, unlawful activity, material policy violation, safety concern or failure to meet marketplace requirements.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated as HarvestPlot develops and as legal, regulatory or marketplace requirements change. The current version published on this page will apply to future use of the platform.

7. Contact

Questions or reports concerning this policy may be submitted through the customer-support channels made available by HarvestPlot.
